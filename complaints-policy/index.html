How to Complain

Email your complaint to: jke.contact.me@gmail.com

We will acknowledge receipt within 3 working days.

We will investigate and aim to respond in writing within 14 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may request further review by a company director.

If you remain dissatisfied and your complaint relates to data privacy, you may refer it to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/
