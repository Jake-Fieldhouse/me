## 1. Purpose

This Agreement is entered into for the purpose of preventing unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information exchanged between the parties in connection with IT services, hardware repair, data destruction, or related consulting engagements.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any data, documentation, or information disclosed by either party, including but not limited to:

- Business data stored on IT equipment
- Personal data subject to UK GDPR
- Technical specifications and system configurations
- Client lists, financial information, and trade secrets
- Any information marked as "Confidential" or reasonably understood to be confidential

---

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
2. Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent
3. Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose stated above
4. Take reasonable measures to protect Confidential Information from unauthorized access
5. Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon request or completion of services

---

## 4. Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to information that:

- Was publicly available at the time of disclosure
- Becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party
- Was independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law or court order

---

## 5. Data Destruction Compliance

Jake Fieldhouse Consulting Ltd confirms adherence to:

- **NIST 800-88 Rev. 1** (Guidelines for Media Sanitization)
- **UK GDPR** (Data Protection Act 2018)
- **Environment Agency Waste Carrier Regulations** (License: CBDL620098)

Certificates of Destruction are provided upon request for all data-bearing media processed.

---
