## 3. Hazard Identification & Control Measures

### 3.1 Physical Hazards

| Hazard | Risk Level | Control Measures |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| **Manual Handling** (lifting equipment) | Medium | Assess load before lifting. Use proper technique (bend knees, keep back straight). Request assistance for items >15kg. Use trolley/sack truck for bulk collections. |
| **Electrical Safety** | Medium | Visual inspection before handling. Do not handle visibly damaged equipment while powered. Isolate power before internal work. PAT-tested equipment only. |
| **Sharps/Broken Equipment** | Low | Wear protective gloves when handling damaged devices. Inspect equipment before packing. Report damaged items to client. |
| **Slips, Trips, Falls** | Low | Assess site conditions on arrival. Keep work area clear. Use appropriate footwear. Report hazards to site manager. |
| **Display Screen Equipment** | Low | Limit continuous screen work to 50-minute intervals during extended diagnostics. Adjust workstation for ergonomic posture. |
| **COSHH (Chemicals)** | Low | Thermal paste, cleaning solvents, and isopropyl alcohol handled with nitrile gloves. MSDS reviewed for unfamiliar products. Adequate ventilation ensured. |
| **Vehicle Loading/Unloading** | Low | Park on level ground. Use vehicle handbrake. Load evenly to maintain balance. Secure items with straps to prevent movement. |
| **Driving for Work** | Medium | Pre-journey vehicle checks (brakes, lights, tyres). Avoid driving when fatigued. Plan routes to avoid congestion. Mobile phone use prohibited while driving. Regular breaks on journeys >2 hours. |
| **Communicable Disease** | Low | Hand sanitizer carried. Face covering available if requested by client. Avoid attendance if symptomatic. |
| **Server Room/Comms Closet** | Low | Monitor ambient temperature. Ensure adequate ventilation. Limit time in confined, high-temperature environments. |

### 3.2 Data & Security Hazards

| Hazard | Risk Level | Control Measures |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| **Data Security** | High | Chain of custody maintained at all times. Equipment secured in locked vehicle. NIST 800-88 sanitization applied to all storage media. |
| **Cybersecurity Incident** | Medium | Report any suspected breach or unauthorised access immediately to client representative. Document incident details. Do not attempt to access client network credentials. Use secure VPN where remote access is required. |
| **Theft/Loss of Equipment** | Medium | Equipment inventoried before and after collection. Unique asset tags or serial numbers recorded. Vehicle secured with alarm when unattended. |

### 3.3 Psychosocial & Wellbeing Hazards

| Hazard | Risk Level | Control Measures |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| **Lone Working** | Medium | Inform office of location and expected duration. Mobile phone carried at all times. Check-in protocol for extended visits (>2 hours). Emergency contact readily available. Controls align with HSE guidance INDG73 (Rev. 2025). |
| **Work-Related Stress** | Low | Manageable workload maintained. Clear communication channels with client. Flexibility to reschedule non-urgent work if capacity exceeded. Access to wellbeing resources via ACAS and Mind charity. |
| **Isolation (Remote/Lone Working)** | Low | Regular client check-ins. Clear boundaries between work and personal time. Professional network maintained for peer support. |

## 4. Dynamic Risk Assessment

This RAMS is supplemented by dynamic risk assessment performed on-site. Additional hazards identified at client premises will be documented and addressed in real-time. Any significant new hazards will be communicated to the client representative immediately.

Where site conditions deviate significantly from those described in this RAMS, work will be paused until appropriate controls are implemented.

## 6. Emergency Procedures

- **Fire:** Evacuate immediately via nearest exit. Do not attempt to retrieve equipment. Raise alarm and call 999 if required.
- **Injury:** Administer first aid if trained and safe to do so. Call 999 for serious injuries. Complete accident report.
- **Spillage:** Report to site manager. Do not handle chemical spills. Evacuate area if fumes present.
- **Security/Cyber Incident:** If data security is compromised, cease operations immediately and notify client representative. Document all relevant details.
- **Personal Safety Concern:** If feeling threatened or unsafe, leave premises immediately and contact emergency services if necessary.
